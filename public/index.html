About

I'm a Level 300 Computer Science student at the University of Business and Integrated Development Studies (UBIDS) in Wa, and a self-taught full-stack developer. I founded PascalLinks, a digital services platform, and StoreLinks, a multi-vendor e-commerce marketplace — both built to solve real problems for people in Ghana, not just to look good on a résumé.

Right now I'm on industrial attachment at Noni Hub in Wa, where I facilitate web development training and work on project builds. Outside of code, I handle the side of the work most developers skip: formatting a clean report in Word, building a dashboard in Excel that a non-technical manager can actually read, or putting together a pitch deck that gets a founder through an investor meeting.

I also build mobile apps with Flutter, help businesses integrate AI into their day-to-day operations, and mentor people who are just starting out — walking beginners through web development, Excel, PowerPoint and Word until they can build things on their own, not just follow along.

That combination — able to build the product, present it, and teach someone else to do it — is what I bring to a team.
